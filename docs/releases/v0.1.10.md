More banking data.

- **Credit limit and interest rate.** Each shared bank account gets a credit limit sensor (where the bank reports one) and a headline interest rate sensor, with every rate and type as attributes ([#29](https://github.com/purcell-lab/ha-openfeed/pull/29)).
- **Transaction totals, opt in.** Turn on **Summarise bank transactions** in the integration options for money in and out this month and over the last 30 days, plus net interest and fees this month. Only totals are kept; descriptions, payees and references are never stored, and nothing is written to long-term statistics.
- **Privacy.** The unmasked BSB and account number from the account detail are dropped on arrival. Diagnostics show banking statuses and counts only.

Energy-only entries are unchanged. To add banking, connect a bank in openfeed, then reconfigure the entry and tick banking.
